I am a M.Sc. student in the Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering at the University of Waterloo, co-supervised by Prof. Bruce MacVicar and Prof. Chul Min Yeum. 

My research lies at the intersection of urban hydrology and computer vision. For my master's thesis, I am developing computer vision-based tools to enable real-time monitoring of urban drainage systems and overland runoff flow velocity to mitigate pluvial flood hazards. 

**I am actively seeking a PhD position starting in Fall 2027**, with a research focus on **smart stormwater systems**. While equipping stormwater systems with sensors has proven effective at improving water quality and managing overland runoff quantity, the scalability and transferability of these systems remain poorly understood. My goal is to develop generalizable frameworks that can be seamlessly deployed across any sites to deliver consistent benefits.
